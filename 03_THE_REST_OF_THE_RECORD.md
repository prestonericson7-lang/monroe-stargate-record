# 03. The third pass: the rest of the record, and what it all means

The third pass, 18 November 2026, done by stepping back to the full scope: the Buckley case read whole rather than from its first session, the redacted and absent papers reached as far as they could be, the program's own numbers found at last, and the whole file read again from the top. What was read is in section 4.

## 1. The rest of the record

**1a. The Buckley case, whole.** Eleven remote-viewing interviews between 21 March and 20 April 1984 at the CIA's request; the CIA set the questions (where he is, how he is, who holds him, later who to contact for release); a photograph taken before his capture cued the first interviews, then his name, date and place of birth, and a photograph "allegedly taken by his captors"; the project "exhausted all current leads" by 20 April and stopped (Summary of Investigation, 14 May 1984, CIA-RDP96-00788R001900530022-1, read whole, released in part; in `documents/`). The CIA, not the viewers, supplied the lead that by the public record was right: on 2 April 1984 a second viewer (source 63) was tasked "to verify a suspected location of an individual ... The project 8404 sponsor suspected that Buckley might be in Baalbek, Lebanon" (HU-1074/8404/63, CIA-RDP96-00788R001900590001-8, 18 pages, the transcript read in the reading room's text up to its agent report; in `documents/`).

That session is the most checkable thing in the whole file. Against the sponsor's site the viewer gave three bearings: "approximately 21 miles north-northwest ... rugged mountain peaks"; "approximately 47 miles north-northeast ... an airfield and a lake"; "approximately 35 miles south-southeast ... a flat area with many white buildings, perhaps a city." Measured from Baalbek (`tools/bearings.py`; coordinates from the places' own Wikipedia entries; great-circle distance; bearings from true north): Qurnat as Sawda', Lebanon's highest peak, 20.9 miles at 345.7 degrees, north-northwest; Lake Homs, 48.7 miles at 25.2 degrees, north-northeast; Damascus, 34.6 miles at 170.2 degrees, which is a degree and a half inside south on the sixteen-point compass and a degree and a half from south-southeast. Three for three, within two miles and a compass point each. Now the other half, from the same transcript: the viewer had been shown "overhead imagery" with "a building that was labeled as suspected" before the session, declared his own perceptions "AOL" (the program's word for memory and analysis leaking into the signal) twice, and the bearings were asked as "movement exercises" from the suspected site. A man who knows a map of Lebanon can put the high peaks to the northwest of Baalbek, the Homs lake to the north-northeast and Damascus to the south-southeast from memory. So the record shows a real match and cannot say where it came from, because the protocol was not sealed. That is exactly the gap tests T1 and T5 in `04` close.

The last session, 20 April 1984 (HU-1080/8404, CIA-RDP96-00788R001900630001-3, 71 pages, the first twenty read; in `documents/`), was an outside source who asked for and was given the photographs, the name, the birth date and place, a map of Beirut with the residence marked, the date and hour of the kidnapping, and a map of Lebanon; the source, who said "I know Arabic" and "I know Lebanon", placed him "within the vicinity of Beirut" but "not the city of Beirut". That session is front-loaded by its own transcript and tells us nothing about the faculty. Buckley died in captivity in 1985 by the public record.

**1b. The DIA years, with numbers at last.** The program's successor at DIA wrote annual production reports. SUN STREAK Annual Report 1988 (CIA-RDP96-00788R001000380004-7, 22 pages, read whole as released, two pages exempt; in `documents/`): remote viewing "at a minimum ... a discipline of last resort that can be utilized as a cuing mechanism"; "About 15 to 20 percent of operational sessions were evaluated based on customer feedback, known ground truth, or public disclosures"; "seventy-four sessions were targeted against the US hostages in Lebanon. Customer feedback indicates the information provided was of value to the customer when it addressed the locations of the hostages, their groupings, and their physical status. Predictive estimates on the dates of release were in error"; "Continuing attempts to provide information of a predictive nature were generally in error notwithstanding some occasional success. To date, the results of our efforts dealing with future time are simply not marketable."

Production Report by Viewer, CY-89 (CIA-RDP96-00789R001100020015-2, 19 pages, read whole as released; in `documents/`; the OCR of its tables is broken in places and only the clearly legible figures are quoted): per viewer, of evaluated sessions, "high" value ran from 3 to 27 percent, "value" from 41 to 69 percent, "low" from 11 to 48 percent; "training reliability" 52 to 82 percent and "operational reliability" 33 to 62 percent where established, by the program's own customer-feedback scoring. These are the only scored numbers in everything read, and they are the program grading itself.

The same report carries the Extended Remote Viewing protocol, which is the direct descendant of Monroe's method inside DIA: its skills list includes "Hemispheric Synchronization" twice, under "increase awareness of internal feelings and images" and "engage a receptive mode right hemispheric functioning", and its goal is "a specified discrete state of consciousness in which the trainee is able to RV". So the "discrete state" of McMoneagle's 1984 training (`01`, document h) became a named protocol and ran at DIA until at least 1989. The 1988 report also carries the Coordinate Remote Viewing protocol with its three training classes, which matter for the ladder in `04`: Class C, immediate positive-only feedback from a monitor who holds the answer; Class B, the monitor knows the site but gives no feedback and may only ask for elaboration of what the viewer already said; Class A, the monitor knows nothing and the viewer gets nothing. And a program called Abstract Referents Discrimination of Binary Alternatives, which opens: "Remote Viewers have demonstrated little ability to discriminate alphanumeric information", and then trains and tests exactly that, in phases, "to a statistically significant level".

**1c. 1978, the first intent.** The GONDOLA WISH memo of 15 May 1978 (CIA-RDP96-00788R002000160003-1, read whole): "Actual training consists of advanced biofeedback techniques coupled with brain hemisphere synchronization"; trainees were to be screened by "preliminary psychological tests to insure their stability during altered states of consciousness"; the first targets were to be "word" information, "computer access passwords", recovered from "nonwitting subjects"; and "At no time will the trainees be placed in altered states of consciousness without the presence of the Gondola Wish project officer ... to insure that while an individual is in an altered state he does not discuss classified information." The ICF request of 19 July 1978 (CIA-RDP96-00788R002000160004-0, read whole) gives the reason for secrecy in one sentence: the funds were used because of "the embarrassment which would be suffered by both the US Army and the training organization, should US Army Intelligence involvement with this type of training be disclosed." The 1 November 1978 GONDOLA WISH staff study (CIA-RDP96-00788R002000160011-2, 8 pages, the first three read) states as an assumption that "The CIA has successfully used parapsychological techniques for offensive intelligence collection" and explains the close hold by the "religious and occult overtones" of the subject and the fear of being "labeled as some kind of a nut". The 1978 pilot's outcome is still not in any document found (gap G2 of `02` stands).

**1d. The verdict from inside, 1992.** A Defense Intelligence College master's thesis of November 1992 (Zarbo, CIA-RDP96-00789R002600250001-6, 97 pages, the abstract and introduction read): "Research in this area is at a standstill in the United States"; the thesis uses "remote viewing", "astral projection" and "out-of-body experience" as synonyms and argues the program was dropped for fear of ridicule rather than for lack of data.

**1e. Monroe's own entry, 1976.** A Who's Who style entry kept in the Army's file (CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210041-7, read whole): vice president of programming at the Mutual Broadcasting System 1954-56, president of Carolina Radio and Southern States Radio, Jefferson Cable, Monroe Industries, director of Whistlefield Research Labs, executive director of the Institute from 1976, married Nancy Penn in February 1971.

## 2. What all of it means in the full scope, plainly

The method is a real way into real states; the Army bought it, kept it secret out of embarrassment by its own words, used it on a man whose life was at stake, and never ran a sealed test. The one checkable session in the file matches the map three for three and is unsealed, so it proves the protocol's weakness, not the faculty. The DIA's own scoring says the product was "of value" about half the time by the customers' word, rarely high, and useless about the future by the program's own admission. Monroe's hemisphere synchronization lived on inside the DIA protocol until the end.

Nobody in any of it had the three things that are cheap today: a target chosen by a machine and sealed by a hash before the session, a number generated after the report so that the only way to be right is the way the program itself conceded does not work, and a chain of records nobody can alter afterward. That is the whole scope, and it is why `04` is short.

## 3. What the record cannot say, said once

The frame of `02` found real seams and no hidden working ability. The plain reading of `01` found a method nobody measured. This pass found that the one checkable session is unsealed and that the program's own scores are modest. None of that settles whether anything happens in these states beyond the states themselves. A sealed test would. Until one is run, every stronger claim in either direction is belief.

The map check: coordinates from the Wikipedia entries of Baalbek, Damascus, Lake Homs, Qurnat as Sawda', Rayak Air Base and Lake Qaraoun; the Syrian airbase near Lake Homs has no coordinates on its entry, so the "airfield" half of the second bearing is not scored.
