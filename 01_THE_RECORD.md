# 01. The record, document by document

The first pass, 18 November 2026. The method: the government's own documents first, read by name and quoted from their own pages; the laboratory record beside them, from the papers' own abstracts; the working frame and the plain reading kept apart; and at the end, the tests that would settle what the documents cannot. Every document named here was read as stated in section 7; nothing is cited from a summary of it.

**The search problem.** The CIA reading room cannot be searched by topic in any useful way. "Monroe Institute" returns 883 items, most of them other Monroes; "mind control" returns nothing that matters. The documents that matter sit in the STARGATE collection under the programs' code names, GONDOLA WISH, GRILL FLAME and CENTER LANE, and under the names of the people. That is how everything below was found.

## 1. Who Robert Monroe was, in the government's words and his own

- Born 30 October 1915 in Wabash, Indiana; BA Ohio State 1937; a radio program writer, director, producer and executive (the Army's information paper of 5 January 1984, CIA-RDP96-00788R001700270004-2). In 1958, "then a New York broadcasting executive, [he] began having experiences that drastically altered his life", leaving his body "via a 'second body'" (the GONDOLA WISH report of 25 August 1978, quoting its interview of him). His book, *Journeys Out of the Body*, 1971, second edition 1977. In the 1960s he built audio patterns to drive brain rhythms, "from rotating disc circuit breakers to sophisticated, custom-built signal generators"; "In May 1975, Monroe received a generic patent for this method" (the same report). The patent's number, US 3,884,218, comes from his biography page, not from the patent itself, which was not opened.
- The Institute: founded in the early 1970s (the 1984 paper), near Charlottesville, Virginia, at Afton and then Faber. His method: sound pulses and "binaural beats" that "apparently create a frequency-following response (FFR)" and synchronize the hemispheres, which he called "Hemi-Sync" (the 1984 paper's own description).
- He died on 17 March 1995 (his biography page; not a government document).
- A change in the record worth seeing: on 27 October 1978 the Pentagon's paper says "Neither Monroe nor the Institute has had any sort of security clearance"; on 5 January 1984 the Army's paper says he "has a DoD SECRET security clearance based on a NAC completed on 18 January 1980."

## 2. The government's record, document by document, in the order they were written

**(a) July 1972, DIA, "Controlled Offensive Behavior - USSR", ST-CS-01-169-72, CIA-RDP96-00788R001300010001-7 (346,000 characters of OCR; read: its table of contents and every sentence naming the key words; not read whole).** The frame the whole Army program later stood on: Soviet work in "telepathic communication, telekinetics, and bionics", whose "major impetus ... is said to come from the Soviet military and the KGB". Its parts include mental suggestion and controlled behavior (hypnosis, "telepathic hypnosis"), sound as a means of altering behavior (infrasonic, sonic and ultrasonic noise), and photographs of Kulagina's psychokinesis; it says "the apport technique and astral projection are examples which will be discussed". Its definitions: bioinformation (telepathy, precognition) and bioenergetics (psychokinesis, "the influence of mind upon matter"). This is the document in which Soviet work on the force and on leaving the body entered the U.S. intelligence record, in 1972.

**(b) 25 August 1978, GONDOLA WISH Assessment Report, 902d Military Intelligence Group, CIA-RDP96-00788R002000160001-3, 6 pages, read whole.** The field office that chose Monroe. Its summary: the office had studied "the intelligence threat posed by Soviet state-of-the-art parapsychology since September 1977" and chose the Monroe Institute of Applied Sciences (MIAS) "to train MI personnel in a parapsychological intelligence collection technique, so that they can mimic Soviet collectors". It defines the out-of-body experience against SRI's remote viewing: in an OOBE "the subject perceives himself to be actually at the target location". It records the CIA's earlier look at Monroe (1973-74, "very casual", no conclusions), that Targ and Puthoff knew him and thought him credible, and that Charles Tart "has conducted investigations of Monroe under laboratory conditions and is convinced of the validity of Monroe's experience". It records the deal: Monroe agreed to train three MI personnel in OOBE in October and November 1978 for $2,000, approved 19 July 1978, as a pilot. Its own reading of the literature, in one sentence: when a subject is relaxed and in an altered state, "parapsychological functioning is enhanced". It states the mechanism as Monroe described it: binaural beats, a frequency-following response, hemisphere synchronization, and that "by monitoring brain wave frequencies and receiving verbal feedback in a laboratory environment, Monroe can generate the OOBE state for individuals who are properly motivated and receptive." That last sentence is a claim, not a measurement, and the report contains no measurement.

**(c) 27 October 1978, Information Paper, GRILL FLAME (U), DAMI-ISH, signed by Major General E. R. Thompson, CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210021-9, 2 pages, read whole.** Two months later the Pentagon's intelligence staff rejected the same plan. Their facts: the technique "involves sensory deprivation, and the playing of rhythmic sounds to induce an 'altered state of consciousness' -- i.e., hypnosis"; in that state "the Subject undergoes some sort of interrogation by Monroe or an assistant"; the CIA found Monroe "not a security risk per se" but "will not permit their people to undergo training at MIAS"; altering anyone's state of mind "comes under a Federal statute which covers experimentation on human beings"; no clearance. Off the record, DIA and CIA people who knew him thought him "a 'nice guy' who is trying to cash in on some unique talent that he alone possesses", and "No one in either agency believes that time or money should be spent on MIAS." So in 1978 the record holds both verdicts, sixty days apart, from the same Army.

**(d) 13 April 1982, Individual Training Requirements - Joe, DET G, CIA-RDP96-00788R001200380001-8, 1 page, read whole.** The command psychologist proposes a visit to the Monroe Institute by "you, Fred, Joe, and myself" and "possible training for him at Monroe's", with "Monroe technology transfer to DET G, if applicable". By the memos around it, Joe is Mr. McMoneagle of the 1984 memos below, and Fred is Atwater.

**(e) 9 June 1983, Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process, LTC Wayne M. McDonnell, US Army Operational Group, INSCOM, CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5, 29 pages, read whole (the archive.org text of the same CIA PDF, checked against the reading room's own page; both texts are in `documents/`).** The famous one. What it is: one officer's attempt, after taking the Gateway training himself, to explain why it works, built almost entirely on Itzhak Bentov's book *Stalking the Wild Pendulum*, plus Pribram and Bohm's holographic mind, in a chain that runs: binaural beats entrain the brain (the FFR); deep rest removes the heart's "bifurcation echo" so the body resonates near 7 Hz with the earth's ionospheric cavity; the mind is a hologram reading the universe's hologram; an oscillation "clicks out" of time-space at its rest points "when the speed of the oscillation drops below 10^-33 centimeters per second (Planck's Distance)"; so a focused consciousness can leave time-space, read the past (Focus 15) and the future (Focus 21), and the out-of-body state is a faster road to all of it. Its conclusion: "There is a sound, rational basis in terms of physical science parameters for considering Gateway to be plausible", followed by a twelve-step program (A to L) for an "organization-wide exploitation", including hypnosis to speed it up, REM-sleep frequencies, and three viewers out of body on one target in the present, the past and the future.

Read plainly, two things stand out. First, there is not one measurement in the twenty-nine pages; the only numbers are Bentov's. Second, the one place it reports a test is paragraph 33, and the test failed: "numerous experiments have been conducted involving persons moving from one coast to the other in the out-of-body state to read a series of ten computer generated numbers in a university laboratory", and "none have ever succeeded in getting all ten correct". The paper explains the misses as holograms seen "pseudoscopically", past energy patterns, and thought forms. That digit test is the Army's own criterion of success, stated by its own analyst, and it is exactly the test that can be run sealed today (`04_SEALED_TESTS.md`, T1). Also on the record there: the REM-sleep observation (a trainer "could guarantee out-of-body movements by bringing participants down into a REM state" and then using the tapes), the "2877.3 CPS" Beta signal on the out-of-body tape, and the estimate that "less than five percent of all participants" reach Focus 15 in a seven-day course. The physics chain does not hold as physics (a length is not a speed; nothing in quantum mechanics says an oscillation leaves spacetime at its turning points), and the paper says of itself that parts of it need "a touch of right brain intuitive insight". Its value is different: it tells us exactly what the Army believed and what it would have counted as proof.

**(f) 5 January 1984, The Monroe Institute of Applied Sciences (U), the Army's information paper for the Secretary of the Army, CIA-RDP96-00788R001700270004-2, 3 pages, read whole.** INSCOM's association with MIAS "dates to 1978, when the potentials of the Hemi-Sync process were considered for application within INSCOM's initial psychoenergetic efforts"; CENTER LANE advanced training with MIAS "consists of four progressive phases that lead to full use of the Hemi-Sync process during CENTER LANE intelligence collection missions"; RAPT, a Hemi-Sync course used to screen prospective sources; CENTEX plans for accelerated language learning. Attached: the Institute's own brochure for the DISCOVERY tapes, citing "Auditory Beats in the Brain", *Scientific American*, September 1973, for the beat phenomenon.

**(g) 6 January 1984, MIAS Briefing to the Secretary of the Army, 5 January 1984 (U), LTC Brian Buzby, CIA-RDP96-00788R001700270003-3, 3 pages, read whole.** Buzby and Atwater briefed Secretary John Marsh for thirty minutes on Monroe, MIAS, RAPT and CENTER LANE, including "the CENTER LANE use of Mr. Monroe in a personal services contract" and how the "one-on-one" training "refined the remote viewing process". The Secretary asked whether the training violated the directives on human experimentation; the answer was informed-consent forms and the DA and DoD legal review that had approved CENTER LANE as a human-use project; "The Secretary added 'good.'" This is the highest level the record shows Monroe's method reaching.

**(h) 28 March 1984, Trip Report - Advanced Training 19-23 Mar 84 (U), CPT Frederick H. Atwater, CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210003-9, 2 pages, read whole; and (i) 29 March 1984, Continuation of MIAS Advanced Training (U), LTC Buzby, CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210002-0, 2 pages, read whole.** The operational record, and the most exact pages in the set. The fourth week of advanced training with McMoneagle; photographs in sealed envelopes as cues; "somatic electrical responses reflected the accuracy of the descriptions" (their claim of a skin-electrical correlate); on 21 March 1984 two operational interviews "using a Hemi-Sync environment of multiple frequencies (100Hz base, 1.5Hz binaural beat and 200Hz base, 4Hz binaural beat)", "the first time operations have been conducted using a Hemi-Sync environment"; on 22 March 1984 the source "achieved what is called 'First Stage Separation'", with "appropriate somatic electrical potentials". Buzby's memo: the July 1983 personal-services contract with Monroe was "to examine the use of his technology (HEMI-SYNC) in the psychoenergetic intelligence collection process", with "better than expected results", continued to train McMoneagle for his retirement as a private consultant and to "determine if the 'discrete state' psychoenergetic technique provides high resolution target information of intelligence value". Note what is and is not here: beat frequencies to the tenth of a hertz, a physiological correlate claimed, and no numbers for either the correlate or the accuracy.

**(j) Undated (about 1980), M.I.A.S. Bulletin #PR31380H, The Hemi-Sync Process, CIA-RDP96-00788R001100200007-2, 2 pages, read whole.** The Institute's own claims, kept in the Army's files: hemispheric synchronization defined as EEG "simultaneously equal in amplitude and frequency"; the Greens' Menninger subject with twenty years of Zen who held it "over fifteen minutes"; the Institute's conclusions "based upon both anecdotal and bio-monitoring consensus from 4,823 experiments among 1,280 volunteer participants over a five year period", a list of effects from relaxation to "permanent memory addition", "verified and replicated consistently both at the Institute and other facilities", and "Formal papers of such effort will be presented to appropriate groups at a future date." No such formal paper was found in this reading.

**(k) 1995, the AIR evaluation that ended the program (not CIA documents; both read from the authors' own texts).** Jessica Utts, "An Assessment of the Evidence for Psychic Functioning" (her PDF): "psychic functioning has been well established", an effect "between what social scientists call a small and medium effect". Ray Hyman's evaluation (his full text): "we agree on many other points"; "the findings of a single laboratory count for nothing unless they can be reliably replicated in other laboratories"; "promising findings did not have the opportunity of being replicated in other laboratories." Both agreed the SAIC experiments showed an effect above chance; they disagreed on whether that is evidence of anything but the experiments.

## 3. The laboratory record, from the papers' own abstracts (read: the abstracts; the full texts not read)

- **Binaural beats and entrainment, the mechanism under Hemi-Sync.** Ingendoh and co-authors, *PLoS One* 2023 (PMID 37205669), a systematic review of fourteen studies: five in line with entrainment, eight contradictory, the rest mixed; the literature "inconclusive at best". So the Army's premise of 1978 is, forty-five years later, an open measurement, not a settled fact either way.
- **The out-of-body state made in a laboratory.** Blanke and co-authors, *Nature* 2002 (PMID 12239558): "repeated induction of this experience by focal electrical stimulation of the brain's right angular gyrus" in one patient, with illusory limb changes and whole-body displacements, read by the authors as a failure to integrate somatosensory and vestibular information. The sensation Monroe described is reproducible on demand by a wire in the brain.
- **Hidden targets in the largest trials.** Parnia and co-authors, AWARE, *Resuscitation* 2014 (PMID 25301715): 2,060 cardiac arrests, 140 survivors interviewed, 2 percent with explicit recall of real events, one "verifiable period of conscious awareness"; AWARE II, *Resuscitation* 2023 (PMID 37423492): 567 arrests, 28 interviews, "Nobody identified the visual image", one of 28 the sound. In both, a hidden target that only a viewer outside the body could see went unseen.
- **The force on a machine.** Boesch, Steinkamp and Boller, *Psychological Bulletin* 2006 (PMID 16822162): 380 studies of intention on random number generators, "a significant but very small overall effect size", effect sizes "strongly and inversely related to sample size", "extremely heterogeneous", and a Monte Carlo showing all three "could in principle be a result of publication bias". The PEAR twelve-year review (Jahn, Dunne, Nelson, Dobyns and Bradish, 1997) was not read; its abstract page did not load.
- Tart's 1968 study of "Miss Z" reading a five-digit number from a shelf, and his 1960s laboratory sessions with Monroe himself, were not read; what is held on them is secondary and is not cited here beyond the Army's own line that Tart was "convinced".

## 4. The working frame and the plain reading

**The frame held open.** The far end of this project, kept as a hypothesis and not a belief: that the states Monroe described are what he said they were, that something like a force acts from a mind on the world, and that the government's interest is itself a kind of evidence. It is held as scope to test. Nothing on these pages is argued from it, and nothing is dismissed because of it.

**The plain reading.** The record supports five plain facts. The Army paid Monroe, used Hemi-Sync in operations with informed consent, and briefed the Secretary of the Army on it; the same Army's staff wrote two months apart that it should and should not; the one objective success criterion its own analyst cites, ten digits read from across the country, was never met; the physiological correlate its officers claimed, a skin-electrical response, was never given a number in these pages; and the Institute's "4,823 experiments" never became a formal paper that could be found. The laboratory record since: the out-of-body sensation is made on demand by stimulating one brain region; the hidden targets in the biggest trials went unseen; the force on random generators is a tiny effect that publication bias alone can produce; the entrainment under Hemi-Sync is unsettled. None of that is a proof of absence, and none is claimed as one. It is the place every honest reading of this kind ends: the only move left is a measurement, and the measurements here are cheap, exact, and were never run sealed by the people who believed. That is what `04_SEALED_TESTS.md` is for.

## 5. What a sealed test can do that nobody did

The gift of cheap exact hardware here is not speed; it is that every random bit can have a hash-chained record, and the sealing cannot be undone by anyone, the experimenter included. Four protocols came out of this first pass, in the order they would be built: **T1, the digits**, the Army's own criterion, sealed; **T2, the force on a machine**, the 1978 "switch triggered by mental processes" (`02`, S8) run at full exactness; **T3, Hemi-Sync made exact**, the Army's own operational beat frequencies as phase-locked audio with the brain's response recorded; **T4, the somatic correlate**, Atwater's skin-voltage claim with a number on it. A fifth, **T5, the future number**, came out of the third pass. All five are written out in `04_SEALED_TESTS.md`.
