# The documents

Every document cited in `01`, `02` and `03`, with its CIA reading-room number, its date, how much of it was read, and whether a copy is kept in this folder. A reading-room page is `https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/` plus the number in lower case; its scanned PDF is `https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/` plus the number in upper case plus `.pdf`. "Whole" means every page as released; "in part" names the part. The reading room's text is its own OCR of the scan and is often broken; the scan is the authority.
